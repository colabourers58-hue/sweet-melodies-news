The Living Waters Singers score their best opening yet with “If There Were Never Any Heaven”

The Ghanaian choir’s new music video drew 10,820 YouTube views in its first 48 hours, ahead of “You’re Invited” and “Only Jesus” at the same age.

  • The video premiered on 11 October 2026 and the single reached streaming services on 14 October.
  • At 48 hours, “You’re Invited” had 10,380 views and “Only Jesus” 6,279. Both passed 50,000 within four weeks.
  • The group has 5,640 YouTube subscribers and 6,700 followers on Instagram.
