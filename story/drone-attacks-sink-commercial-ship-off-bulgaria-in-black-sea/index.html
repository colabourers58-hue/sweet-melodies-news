Drone attacks sink commercial ship off Bulgaria in Black Sea

A second vessel was damaged in Black Sea strikes, raising concerns over vital trade lanes that supply grain to global markets.

WhatsAppX Facebook

Two commercial ships have been hit by drones off Bulgaria in the Black Sea, leaving one vessel submerged.

Why it matters

Disruptions to Black Sea maritime routes threaten vital grain exports that help feed households globally and stabilise food prices. For families and traders relying on stable import costs, escalating tension in key shipping corridors directly impacts the price of basic commodities.

The details

Al Jazeera reported that strikes took place near Bulgarian waters at around 01:30 GMT on Tuesday. Prime Minister Rumen Radev stated that rescue teams pulled one crew member from the vessel that sank, according to the BBC.

The second ship, Chelsea 3, is Ukrainian-owned and registered under a Maltese flag, Al Jazeera reported. All 15 crew members, comprising seven Ukrainians and eight Azerbaijanis, were safely brought ashore.

The BBC reported that five crew members from Chelsea 3 received medical care for minor injuries after three drones struck the ship 18 nautical miles from Byala. Details regarding the sunken vessel remain unannounced.

Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha warned that attacks on Black Sea trade routes put global food security at risk by threatening grain transportation, as reported by the BBC.

Prime Minister Radev summoned Russian Ambassador Eleonora Mitrofanova, remarking that the situation favors Russia despite a lack of direct proof, according to the BBC. Mitrofanova told local media she had no information from Moscow and suggested possible third-party involvement.

The BBC reported that European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen denounced the strikes on social media and backed Bulgarian rescue operations. Prime Minister Radev said he would consult NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte about the situation.

The strikes occurred a week after drone attacks sank another commercial ship named Alfa Watan in the region, leaving 10 crew members missing, the BBC reported. Reuters also noted missing sailors following Black Sea sinkings.

What’s next

Bulgarian Prime Minister Rumen Radev is scheduled to hold talks with NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte to discuss security along the Black Sea coast.
