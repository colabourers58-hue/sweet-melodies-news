Ghana offers diplomatic support for Assemblies of God world congress

Missions in 73 countries will assist international delegates arriving in Accra for the global gathering this week.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Ghanaian embassies and high commissions across 73 countries will provide protocol support to delegates attending the World Assemblies of God Congress in Accra, foreign affairs officials confirmed.

Why it matters

The hosting marks the first time an African nation receives the international Christian gathering, bringing foreign visitors to Greater Accra. Local church members, hospitality workers, and traders expect increased activity and smooth entry for international guests.

The details

MyJoyOnline reported that Foreign Affairs Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa gave the commitment after a high-level meeting held at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the Greater Accra Region.

The minister held discussions with a delegation of church leaders led by Rev. Dr Stephen Yenusom Wengam, who serves as the General Superintendent of Assemblies of God in Ghana.

The engagement focused on diplomatic, protocol, and logistical preparations for the international religious conference taking place later this week.

Ghana holds the distinction of being the first nation in Africa to receive the privilege of hosting the global gathering, according to MyJoyOnline.

The foreign ministry’s involvement will assist event organisers as they prepare to welcome participants travelling to the country for the congress.

MyJoyOnline quoted Mr Ablakwa as saying: “The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and all our embassies and high commissions across 73 countries are delighted to be offering diplomatic and protocol support, as we have done for many organisations, and consistent with our traditions.”

The minister explained that Ghana remains dedicated to supporting international bodies in line with long-standing diplomatic traditions. He expressed his best wishes for a successful and life-transforming gathering, adding that the congress offers the country an opportunity to showcase its warmth, stability, and democratic credentials to the world, MyJoyOnline reported.

In a separate report, the Ghana News Agency stated that the global chairman of Assemblies of God has already arrived in Ghana ahead of the world congress.

What’s next

Diplomatic officials and organisers will assist arriving international participants across the week as the congress opens in Accra.
