Kofi Adjorlolo explains absence from LilWin movie project

The veteran actor addresses why he could not feature in the Ghana-Nigeria film production A Journey to Africa.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Veteran actor Kofi Adjorlolo has explained the reason for his absence from Kwadwo Nkansah LilWin’s latest film production.

Why it matters

The statement provides clarity to film fans across Ghana regarding Kofi Adjorlolo’s role in LilWin’s project. It highlights ongoing developments in the joint Ghanaian and Nigerian film collaboration.

The details

GhanaWeb first reported on Friday, 9 October, that Adjorlolo addressed why he was not part of the cast for the new movie.

The film project, titled A Journey to Africa, is a joint production between filmmakers from Ghana and Nigeria, according to Pulse Ghana.

Adomonline.com reported that the cross-border venture features creative talent from both countries under the leadership of actor and producer LilWin.

Adjorlolo clarified his non-involvement following public interest regarding his absence from the cast list, Pulse Ghana noted.

GhanaWeb, Adomonline.com and Pulse Ghana all published details of Adjorlolo’s explanation on Friday, 9 October.

The reporting outlets did not disclose further details regarding official release dates or additional schedules for the movie production.

What’s next

The reports did not specify release dates or additional production schedules for the feature film.
