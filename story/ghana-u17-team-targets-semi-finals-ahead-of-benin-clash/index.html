Ghana U17 team targets semi-finals ahead of Benin clash

A victory against Benin will guarantee the Black Starlets a place in the last four of the WAFU Zone B Championship.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Ghana’s Black Starlets play Benin today in Ouagadougou in their second group match at the 2026 WAFU Zone B U17 Championship.

Why it matters

For Ghanaian football fans and families, youth tournaments showcase the country’s upcoming talent on the international stage. Success in this regional competition also grants direct passage to the 2027 U17 Africa Cup of Nations.

The details

The Ghanaian youth team currently sits at the top of Group A after opening the tournament with a 1-0 victory against Togo. Striker David Kwarteng scored the only goal of that encounter in the seventh minute.

Benin enters the fixture with one point after holding tournament hosts Burkina Faso to a goalless draw in their initial match. A win for Ghana in this second game will guarantee a spot in the semi-finals.

Head Coach Kassim Ocansey shared mixed feelings about the team’s opening performance, as reported by Ghanaian Times. Although happy with the three points, he felt the players should have scored more goals from the chances created.

“We created good chances, but we couldn’t convert,” Mr Ocansey stated after the Togo game. He added that properly converting opportunities could yield three or four goals in upcoming fixtures.

Speaking to Adom News, Assistant Coach Hamza Mohammed acknowledged the threat posed by Benin. He stressed that while the coaching staff respects their opponents, the squad remains focused on achieving its target.

Mr Mohammed told 3News that securing a victory in the first fixture was vital to ease pressure on the squad. He explained that the team plans to build on those early positive elements.

The tournament carries additional weight because only the two finalists in Ouagadougou will earn qualification for the 2027 U17 Africa Cup of Nations.

What’s next

Ghana will seek to secure a spot in the tournament finals to qualify for the 2027 U17 Africa Cup of Nations.
