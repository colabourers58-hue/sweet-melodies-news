Pope Leo XIV to undergo lung surgery in Rome

Catholic leader Pope Leo XIV confirms doctors caught a lung growth early and expects to recover within a week.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Pope Leo XIV announced on Sunday that doctors will perform an operation in coming days to extract a small growth found on his lung.

Why it matters

For Catholic faithful and churchgoers across Ghana, this announcement brings a call to prayer for the health of the 71-year-old head of the global church. The pontiff’s quick recovery ensures that his upcoming international ministry, including planned trips to South America, will proceed without interruption.

The details

The 71-year-old head of the Catholic Church shared the update while addressing pilgrims in St Peter’s Square in Rome, BBC World reported. He explained that caregivers spotted an early growth during a recent check-up and advised surgical removal.

Reassuring worshippers, the pontiff said medical staff caught the mass “at an early stage” and found it “well-contained”. He thanked listeners for their “spiritual closeness” and asked believers to keep him in prayer prior to the procedure.

Matteo Bruni, who speaks for the Holy See Press Office, informed reporters that the procedure is set for early next week. He noted that the head of the church should need around seven days to heal.

Vatican News reported that the surgical team will operate at Gemelli Hospital in Rome. That facility has kept a reserved suite for papal care since the May 1981 attempt on Pope John Paul II’s life.

Formerly named Robert Francis Provost, the current pope entered the priesthood in 1982 and previously carried out missionary work alongside needy communities in Peru. He took office in 2025 following Pope Francis, becoming the 267th Catholic pontiff and the first from America.

Despite his upcoming admission to hospital, the pontiff confirmed that his upcoming trip across Latin America remains intact. BBC World stated that his scheduled tour of Uruguay, Argentina and Peru this November will proceed as planned.

Following the announcement, Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni expressed her “personal solidarity” with the church leader on social media as he prepares for the procedure.

What’s next

The operation will take place in Rome early next week, with the pontiff expected to spend about seven days recovering in hospital.
