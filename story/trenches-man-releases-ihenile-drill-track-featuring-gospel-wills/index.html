Trenches Man releases IHENILE drill track featuring Gospel Wills

Entertainment outlet Six9ja reported the new music collaboration featuring Gospel Wills.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Entertainment platform Six9ja reported that artist Trenches Man has released a new track titled IHENILE DRILL.

Why it matters

Entertainment outlet Six9ja reported the release of a new drill track titled IHENILE by Trenches Man. The song features Gospel Wills as a collaborative artist.

The details

According to the publication by Six9ja, the new musical track features artist Gospel Wills as a collaborative performer alongside primary artist Trenches Man.

Six9ja specified the full title of the song as IHENILE DRILL, indicating that the project is presented in a drill musical style.

The publication carried the track release update on its online media platform as a new song announcement.

Six9ja included no direct statements or quotes from either Trenches Man or Gospel Wills concerning the background or production of the track.

The outlet did not disclose details regarding record label management, sound engineering credits, or planned music video releases for IHENILE DRILL.

Further information regarding song availability on digital streaming platforms was not included in the report provided by Six9ja.
