GRNMA pledges legal support to nurse facing false news trial

Nurse Salomey Awiti Baffoe has pleaded not guilty to abetment charges linked to online video distribution in the Bono Region.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

The Ghana Registered Nurses and Midwives Association has promised ongoing legal support for Nurse Salomey Awiti Baffoe as she defends herself against charges in court.

Why it matters

The nursing association’s decision underscores its policy of defending member welfare even when court charges do not involve professional health duties. For public sector workers across Ghana, it demonstrates the legal backing available through registered trade unions during court trials.

The details

Adom News reported that the national president of the health union, Perpetual Ofori-Ampofo, personally delivered this pledge during a face-to-face visit with Nurse Baffoe in the Bono Region.

The meeting occurred on the opening day of the Phase Three Regional Tour being carried out by the National Executive Committee of the nursing group.

According to the news report, Mrs Ofori-Ampofo explained that union leadership has carefully monitored the court case from the beginning and has secured legal representation for the nurse throughout the judicial process.

The association president emphasized that although the criminal allegations against Nurse Baffoe are entirely separate from her professional nursing responsibilities, the union remains dedicated to protecting the overall interests and welfare of its membership.

Adom News stated that the association affirmed it would continue providing all necessary legal aid as the judicial proceedings advance through the legal system.

The report indicated that Nurse Baffoe is facing a legal charge of abetment of crime, specifically concerning the publication of false news under sections twenty and two hundred and eight of Act 29.

State attorneys link the prosecution to accusations that she helped Barbara Asantewaa Kodua, who goes by the name Ghana Jollof, in sharing video recordings and recruiting individuals to distribute the material.

Adom News noted that Nurse Baffoe has entered a plea of not guilty to the charge, with the legal claims yet to be established before the court during the ongoing regional tour.

What’s next

The nursing association confirmed it will maintain legal representation for Nurse Baffoe as the court case continues.
