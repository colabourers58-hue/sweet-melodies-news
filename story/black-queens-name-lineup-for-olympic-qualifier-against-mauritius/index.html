Black Queens Name Lineup For Olympic Qualifier Against Mauritius

Coach Kim Lars Björkegren urges improved finishing as Ghana hosts Mauritius in an LA 2028 Olympic qualifying match.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Black Queens head coach Kim Lars Björkegren has named a strong starting lineup for Ghana’s 2028 Olympic Games qualifying second leg fixture against Mauritius.

Why it matters

The qualification match gives Ghanaian sports supporters across the country and in the diaspora an opportunity to track national team progress toward the 2028 Olympic Games. Hosting international fixtures at Essipong brings major sporting action directly to football fans in the Western Region.

The details

Adomonline, Modern Ghana, and GhanaSoccernet reported that Björkegren instructed the national team to show improved finishing and stay focused in front of goal.

Modern Ghana reported that the coach said, “We must be clinical against Mauritius,” while GhanaSoccernet added that he urged players to sharpen their attack.

The Ghana Football Association and Graphic Online both reported that the technical team selected a strong starting XI for the qualifier.

Africa Top Sports reported that the match takes place at Essipong in the Western Region as Mauritius visit for the Olympic qualification tie.

Ghana Sports Page reported that live coverage was made available for fans following the second leg fixture of the LA 2028 qualification series.

Africa Top Sports added that the Black Queens are focused on securing progress in the qualification campaign through a strong home performance.

What’s next

The Black Queens face Mauritius in the second leg fixture as they aim to advance to the next stage of the LA 2028 Olympic qualifiers.
