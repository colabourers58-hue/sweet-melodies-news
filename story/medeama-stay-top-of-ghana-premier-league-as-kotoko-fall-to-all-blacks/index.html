Medeama stay top of Ghana Premier League as Kotoko fall to All Blacks

Matchday six results saw Hearts of Oak, Bechem United and Berekum Chelsea secure victories on Sunday, 11 October 2026.

Medeama stayed top of the Ghana Premier League table after defeating Heart of Lions on Sunday, 11 October 2026.

Why it matters

Medeama maintain their lead at the top of the Ghana Premier League following Sunday’s matchday six fixtures. The latest round of games brings updated results for football fans across Ghana following matches nationwide.

The details

According to a matchday six roundup published by Adomonline.com, Medeama defeated Heart of Lions to maintain their position at the top of the 2026/27 Ghana Premier League standings.

Adomonline.com also reported that Asante Kotoko lost their week six match against All Blacks. The report noted that the result was one of the central outcomes of the weekend fixtures across the domestic league.

GhanaSoccernet reported that Hearts of Oak recorded a 1-0 victory against Port City in their week six match. Modern Ghana also reported on the encounter between Hearts of Oak and Port City on Sunday, 11 October 2026.

In another week six encounter, Bechem United scored four goals to defeat Vision FC 4-2, according to match details published by GhanaSoccernet. GhanaSoccernet also reported that Berekum Chelsea secured a 3-0 home victory against Debibi United.

GhanaSoccernet reported that Gold Stars earned three points after defeating Basake Holy Stars 2-0. The match formed part of the full slate of matchday six fixtures reported by the sports website on Sunday evening.

According to GhanaSoccernet, FC AshantiGold claimed a 1-0 win over FC Samartex in their week six clash. The sports site also reported that Young Apostles earned a 1-0 victory against Aduana FC.

The match reports published by Adomonline.com, GhanaSoccernet and Modern Ghana document the complete results of matchday six for football fans, families and supporters following the 2026/27 season across Ghana.
