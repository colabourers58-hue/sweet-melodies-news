Ghanaian actor LilWin wept while describing challenges he experienced while working with Nigerian movie stars during a film shoot, GhanaWeb reported.

Why it matters

Cross-border film collaborations between Ghana and Nigeria are a key part of the regional movie industry. Reports of friction on set highlight ongoing operational challenges facing West African actors and producers.

The details

According to GhanaWeb, the popular performer became emotional while sharing details about his personal experiences during the film production.

The news outlet reported that the challenges occurred during a collaborative shoot involving Nigerian film stars.
