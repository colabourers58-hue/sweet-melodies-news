Pray.com launches AI sermon video service for pastors

Churches can now convert sermon messages into cinematic videos using artificial intelligence technology.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Faith platform Pray.com has launched a new service that generates artificial intelligence cinematic videos from pastor sermons, The Christian Post reported.

Why it matters

For church leaders, local congregations and diaspora believers, this tool offers an accessible way to convert traditional audio sermons into visual content. With subscription costs starting at nineteen dollars a month, smaller ministries can utilize high-quality digital media production.

The details

The platform, which previously created viral artificial intelligence Bible videos, is now extending its technology to church leaders.

According to The Christian Post, the system produces cinematic visual content based on sermon audio and teachings provided by ministers.

Pricing for the artificial intelligence studio service begins at a monthly subscription rate of 19 dollars.

The Christian Post reported that the platform aims to assist congregations in converting standard sermon presentations into digital visual media.
