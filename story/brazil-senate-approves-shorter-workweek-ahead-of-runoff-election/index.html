Brazil Senate Approves Shorter Workweek Ahead of Runoff Election

A new Quaest poll shows Flavio Bolsonaro with a slight lead over President Lula in Brazil’s presidential race.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Brazil’s Senate has approved a shorter workweek, giving President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva a major legislative victory ahead of the upcoming election runoff.

Why it matters

The passage of a shorter workweek in the Senate gives President Lula a major legislative victory before voters make their final choice. However, a fresh Quaest poll showing Flavio Bolsonaro with a slight lead indicates that the presidential runoff remains closely contested.

The details

AP News reported that the approval of the shorter workweek by senators represents a major win for President Lula as the country prepares for the presidential vote.

The parliamentary victory for the government comes as political polling shows a tight presidential contest, according to Reuters.

Reuters reported that a poll conducted by Quaest shows candidate Flavio Bolsonaro holding a slight lead over President Lula in the runoff.

The legislative approval in the Senate delivers a key policy victory for President Lula’s administration during the national election campaign.

The survey figures from Quaest highlight the close competitive race between Flavio Bolsonaro and President Lula as voters prepare for the decisive voting round.
