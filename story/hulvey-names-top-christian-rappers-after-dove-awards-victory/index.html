Hulvey names top Christian rappers after Dove Awards victory

Award-winning musician Hulvey shares his top influences and tour goals following his victory at the 2026 Dove Awards.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

American gospel artist Hulvey named Lecrae, Trip Lee, and Andy Mineo as his top three Christian rap musicians during an interview with Rapzilla at the 2026 Dove Awards.

Why it matters

The comments highlight how Christian hip hop continues to reach new listeners and expand its influence internationally. For fans and gospel music lovers across Ghana, his journey reflects the growing global reach of faith-based urban music.

The details

Rapzilla reported that the artist secured the Christian Rap Album of the Year award before sharing details about his career and personal choices.

Speaking about his former Reach Records labelmates, the artist named Lecrae, Trip Lee, and Andy Mineo as the main heavyweights in the genre. He also revealed preferences for text messaging over phone calls, paper Bibles over digital applications, and Waffle House over Cracker Barrel.

Reflecting on his catalog, the recording artist named “Ready or Not” as his worst track. He further described Jack Johnson’s song “Upside Down” as the best track ever created.

On his professional ambitions, the musician stated that expanding live tours remains his primary goal. He explained to Rapzilla that growing his tour presence helps bring new listeners to Christian hip hop.

Hulvey said many followers told him his music provided their first exposure to faith-based rap. He expressed a desire for more listeners to encounter God through his musical work.

Outside of music, the artist discussed his passion for Spider-Man, praising “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” for its storytelling and visual style while praising Tom Holland’s performance.

Hulvey encouraged fans to support the broader Christian hip hop community by sharing music online and buying concert tickets, highlighting performers such as Kijan Boone, Aha Gazelle, and kappa.

What’s next

Hulvey urged supporters to buy concert tickets and share music from emerging Christian hip hop artists including Kijan Boone and Aha Gazelle.
