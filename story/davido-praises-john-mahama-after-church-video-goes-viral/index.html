Davido praises John Mahama after church video goes viral

The Nigerian musician commended the Ghanaian leader’s humility after watching a trending video of him dancing in church.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Nigerian musician Davido has commended Ghanaian President John Mahama following the online release of a viral video that showed the political leader dancing during a church service.

Why it matters

Public praise from a prominent African artist highlights how church moments shape public perception of political leaders. For Christian viewers, it emphasizes the cultural value placed on humility during divine worship.

The details

Legit.ng reported that the Nigerian recording artist publicly shared his assessment of the Ghanaian leader’s character after watching footage that documented his conduct during the Christian church service.

According to Pulse Ghana, the music star expressed public admiration for the president, specifically praising him for demonstrating humility while dancing alongside fellow churchgoers during praise and worship.

Pulse Ghana noted that the video clip showing the Ghanaian leader actively participating in the church service quickly gained wide popularity among social media users across the West African region.

Legit.ng stated that the recording of the president’s church conduct trended extensively on major social media platforms prior to the Nigerian artist offering his public remarks on the matter.

Pulse Ghana further reported that public attention focused on the modest manner in which the leader conducted himself inside the sanctuary, as the viral video continued to accumulate views.

According to reports from Legit.ng, the video clip generated broad digital engagement, prompting online users to comment on the Ghanaian president’s willingness to dance publicly during divine worship.

Pulse Ghana reported that the positive comments from the Nigerian musician added to the growing public interest in how political leaders carry themselves when attending Christian church gatherings.

Both Legit.ng and Pulse Ghana confirmed that the viral video recording of the church service served as the direct inspiration for the musician’s public tribute to the Ghanaian president.

What’s next

The sources have not announced any further statements or scheduled events regarding the viral video.
