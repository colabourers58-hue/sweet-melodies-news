Scientology Network broadcasts World Mental Health Day marathon

The global television event highlights issues around psychiatric practices and mental health rights.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Scientology Network broadcast a documentary marathon on Saturday, 10 October, to mark World Mental Health Day, according to a press release.

Why it matters

Mental health awareness remains a crucial topic for families, students, and healthcare advocates across Ghana. This international broadcast offers global perspectives on psychiatric regulations and human rights protections in mental health care.

The details

The special broadcast highlighted the activities of the Citizens Commission on Human Rights, an international nonprofit mental health watchdog established in 1969. The press release noted that the group has helped enact nearly 400 laws designed to protect citizens against abusive or coercive practices within mental healthcare systems.

The programming schedule commenced at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Saturday. PR Newswire reported that the marathon aimed to examine the psychiatric industry, focusing on public concerns surrounding alleged links between psychiatric medications, suicide, aggression, and violent actions.

Viewers in Ghana and around the world can access the broadcasts via satellite services and digital streaming platforms, including mobile applications and smart television devices. The network stated that its global broadcasts currently reach audiences in over 240 countries and territories across 17 languages.

David Miscavige, the ecclesiastical leader of the Scientology religion, originally launched the television platform on 12 March 2018. The channel operates directly from Scientology Media Productions, which serves as the organisation’s primary global production facilities in Los Angeles, California.

Besides content covering Scientology programs, the network airs documentaries produced by independent filmmakers from diverse cultural and faith backgrounds who seek to uplift local communities. The press release added that the platform has secured over 175 media industry awards, including Telly, Communitas, and Hermes Creative honours.

The network indicated that complete broadcast schedules and educational resources regarding mental health rights remain available online through its designated websites, cchr.org and scientology.tv.

What’s next

Viewers can access full broadcast schedules and educational resources on the network’s official websites.
