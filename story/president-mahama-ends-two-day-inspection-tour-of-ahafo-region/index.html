President Mahama ends two-day inspection tour of Ahafo Region

The President inspected health facilities, road projects and a major poultry processing facility in Bechem.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

President John Dramani Mahama has concluded a two-day inspection tour of development projects across the Ahafo Region.

Why it matters

The Ahafo Region projects will expand local primary healthcare access and create agricultural jobs for local poultry farmers. Increased domestic poultry production and new road rehabilitation aim to lower food costs and improve regional transportation.

The details

The Ghana News Agency first reported that President Mahama inspected the ongoing construction of the Poultry, Meat and Feed Processing Plant in Bechem. Graphic Online reported that the project is halfway complete following sod-cutting in November 2025. President Mahama stated that the facility would turn Bechem into the country’s primary chicken processing hub once operational.

According to MyJoyOnline, Food and Agriculture Minister Eric Opoku confirmed the factory will feature two processing lines with a combined capacity of 6,000 eggs hourly. Mr Opoku noted that local poultry output rose from under five per cent to 22 per cent by May 2026. He added that the government plans to add eggs to the School Feeding Programme to absorb local surpluses.

Graphic Online reported that the President also inspected new Community-Based Health Planning and Services facilities at Ahyiaem and Nsuapemkrom in the Tano North Municipality. These health centers were funded through the District Assemblies Common Fund, with eighty per cent of allocations sent directly to local assemblies. President Mahama announced that patients at these compounds receive care free of charge under the Free Primary Healthcare Programme.

Adom News reported that the President addressed traditional leaders and citizens at Goaso to wrap up the tour. President Mahama pledged additional road improvements, including asphalting work on the Tepa-Mabang-Goaso stretch and the Sunyani-Ntotroso-Acherensua road. Paramount Chief Barima Twereko Ampem commended the government for addressing transportation challenges across the area.

Roads Minister Governs Kwame Agbodza stated that several finished road projects will be commissioned before the end of 2026. Agriculture officials expect the Bechem poultry plant to be fully operational by the end of March 2027.

What’s next

Roads Minister Governs Kwame Agbodza said several completed regional roads will be commissioned before the end of 2026. The Bechem poultry plant is scheduled to become fully operational by the end of March 2027.
