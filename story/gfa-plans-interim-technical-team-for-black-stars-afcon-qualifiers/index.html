GFA plans interim technical team for Black Stars AFCON qualifiers

The Ghana Football Association will rely on temporary coaches for November’s 2027 AFCON qualifying matches against Somalia.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

The Ghana Football Association will choose temporary technical handlers for the Black Stars on Friday, 9 October.

Why it matters

The decision gives national team supporters clarity on who will direct the technical bench during upcoming international fixtures. It also allows football administrators additional time to secure a permanent head coach for the national team.

The details

Citi Sports Online reported that the national football body resolved to create a temporary technical team to direct the senior national squad during the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying matches against Somalia.

According to Africa Top Sports, football administrators decided to delay appointing a full-time head coach, opting instead for a short-term arrangement to manage the team during the November qualifiers.

GhanaWeb reported that Ibrahim Tanko and Godwin Attram were selected to direct the Black Stars from the bench during the upcoming qualification fixtures against Somalia.

GhanaSoccernet reported that the temporary technical leaders will guide the senior national team during a two-leg qualifying double-header against Somalia in November.

MyJoyOnline and Adomonline.com both reported that the short-term coaching staff will assume responsibility for all Black Stars fixtures scheduled for the November international window.

Modern Ghana reported that this temporary leadership setup will remain active for the upcoming Somalia matches while administrators evaluate options for a permanent technical head.

What’s next

The interim technical team will take charge of the squad ahead of the November 2027 AFCON qualifiers against Somalia.
