Kofi Adams denies Sports Ministry role in World Cup visa controversy

An aide says the National Sports Authority, Tourism Authority and TRIBE Culture Fest had separate embassy arrangements.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

An aide to the Sports Minister, Kofi Adams, has denied that the Ministry of Youth and Sports was involved in the 2026 World Cup visa scandal.

Why it matters

The clarification offers transparency to Ghanaian travelers, students, and workers seeking clarity on government travel procedures for international tournaments. It helps families and diaspora communities understand how state bodies handle embassy arrangements during major events.

The details

According to CitiNewsroom.com, the minister’s aide stated that the TRIBE Culture Fest, the National Sports Authority, and the Ghana Tourism Authority each conducted separate visa arrangements with the United States Embassy.

CitiNewsroom.com reported that these three entities managed their diplomatic outreach independently, ensuring that each institutional application process remained separate from the others during the international travel preparation.

Ghana Sports Online reported that the Ministry of Youth and Sports provided an official clarification detailing the timeline of engagements involving the National Sports Authority and the TRIBE Culture Fest during the entire process.

Ghana Sports Online reported on the ministry’s timeline clarification, followed by additional media coverage from Modern Ghana, Adomonline.com, and CitiNewsroom.com as the story developed across the nation.

Modern Ghana reported that Mr Adams explicitly rejected allegations linking the ministry to the visa issues, maintaining that official ministry channels were not involved in any questionable practices.

Adomonline.com also reported the formal denial from Mr Adams, who emphasized that the sports ministry carried no responsibility for the individual visa arrangements that led to recent public scrutiny.

For Ghanaian churchgoers, families, workers, students, traders, and diaspora members, these official statements clarify how various public institutions and event organizers managed their dealings with foreign embassies in the Greater Accra Region and across the country.

The source reports did not state whether further official investigations or public parliamentary hearings will be conducted regarding the separate visa arrangements made by the organizations.

What’s next

The news reports did not name any upcoming dates or scheduled decisions regarding the visa arrangements.
