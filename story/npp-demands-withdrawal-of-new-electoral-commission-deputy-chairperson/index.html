NPP demands withdrawal of new Electoral Commission deputy chairperson

The opposition party alleges political bias, while government argues the appointment restores balance to the electoral body.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

The New Patriotic Party has demanded the immediate withdrawal of Emelia Afi Agbenyo following her swearing-in as Deputy Chairperson of the Electoral Commission in the Greater Accra Region.

Why it matters

For Ghanaian voters, public trust in the Electoral Commission is essential to ensure peaceful and credible elections. Disagreements over key appointments raise concerns about public confidence in future voter registration and election results.

The details

President John Dramani Mahama swore in Ms Agbenyo alongside Dr Joseph Kwaku Asamoah on Monday, 12 October 2026. The two new officials fill positions left vacant after former deputy commissioners Dr Bossman Eric Asare and Samuel Tettey departed from office.

MyJoyOnline reported that President Mahama urged both appointees during their swearing-in ceremony to perform their constitutional obligations independently. He stressed that no political candidate or party should receive any preferential treatment from the commission.

Adom News reported that NPP National Communications Director Dennis Miracles Aboagye released an official party statement on Tuesday, 13 October 2026. The statement called on President Mahama to revoke Ms Agbenyo’s appointment without delay.

The opposition party argued that her involvement in political rallies and previous role as governance spokesperson for the NDC manifesto team could damage public trust in future voter registration, constituency boundary decisions, and final election results.

Government spokesperson Felix Kwakye Ofosu admitted on Joy FM’s Top Story program that the governing party intended to tilt the balance within the electoral body. He asserted that previous appointments under the former administration had created a political imbalance among the commissioners.

Mr Kwakye Ofosu defended Ms Agbenyo’s academic and professional qualifications, stating that her actual conduct while in office will address public concerns about her neutrality. He added that the opposition party had also appointed people with known political ties during its time in power.

According to Adom News, NDC Director of Elections Mahdi Jibril questioned why opposition criticism focused exclusively on Ms Agbenyo while ignoring Dr Asamoah. He urged all political parties to concentrate their efforts on training election agents rather than debating commissioner backgrounds.

3News reported that NPP Second Vice Chairman Henry Nana Boakye announced a National Steering Committee meeting for Wednesday, 14 October 2026, to determine further steps. Meanwhile, Yen.com.gh reported that the party is weighing legal options before the Supreme Court.

What’s next

The NPP National Steering Committee scheduled a meeting on Wednesday, 14 October to determine its next steps, while party leadership indicated it is considering legal action at the Supreme Court.
