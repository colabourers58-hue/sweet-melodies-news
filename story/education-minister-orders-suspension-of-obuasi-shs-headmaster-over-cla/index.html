Education Minister orders suspension of Obuasi SHS headmaster over claims

The Ghana Education Service and police have been asked to investigate Zakaria Abdulai following a media report.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Education Minister Haruna Iddrisu has directed the Ghana Education Service to immediately suspend Obuasi Senior High Technical School headmaster Zakaria Abdulai over alleged sexual misconduct.

Why it matters

For parents across Ghana who entrust their children to secondary schools, the directive highlights official responses to student safety concerns. It shows how authorities address allegations involving school heads to protect pupils in the Ashanti Region and across the country.

The details

According to MyJoyOnline, this directive from the education minister follows an investigative report produced by media outlet JoyNews and journalist Manasseh Azure Awuni regarding allegations of misconduct involving a female student at the school in the Ashanti Region.

Adom News reported that the Ministry of Education instructed top executive officers at the Ghana Education Service to move swiftly to interdict Mr Abdulai pending comprehensive investigations into the situation.

In an official statement released on Tuesday, 13 October 2026, Press Secretary to the Minister Hashmin Mohammed noted that sector leadership considers the reported matter exceptionally serious and demanding of immediate administrative action, 3News reported.

Graphic Online reported that Mr Iddrisu has also formally written to the police Criminal Investigation Department headquarters in Accra, asking officers to commence a thorough investigation into the allegations.

According to 3News, both the Ministry of Education and state education authorities reaffirmed their shared position that improper behavior toward students will not be tolerated in any educational facility across Ghana.

MyJoyOnline noted that official correspondence urged teachers to remember that families hand over pupils to schools expecting educators to protect young people rather than mistreat them.

As reported by 3News, ministry officials emphasized that the administrative interdiction aims to give room for unhindered inquiries, stressing that all claims are under review and no conclusions of wrongdoing have been reached.

What’s next

The Criminal Investigation Department of the Ghana Police Service and the Ghana Education Service will conduct full inquiries into the allegations.
