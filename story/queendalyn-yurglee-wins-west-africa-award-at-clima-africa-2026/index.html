Queendalyn Yurglee wins West Africa award at CLIMA Africa 2026

Piesie Esther and Scott Evans also picked up continental gospel music honours for Ghana.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Ghanaian gospel musician Queendalyn Yurglee has won the West Africa regional artiste category at the 2026 CLIMA Africa honours.

Why it matters

The awards showcase the growing reach of Ghanaian gospel music and Christian ministry across Africa and Europe. For local churchgoers and upcoming gospel artistes, these honours highlight how Ghanaian praise culture continues to inspire believers internationally.

The details

Gospel Hypers reported that the award represents her second straight year of recognition at the continental gospel presentation. In 2025, she took home the award for Africa’s most promising artist at the ceremony held in Johannesburg, South Africa.

The latest prize comes after the release of her ten-track debut album titled Clouds of Glory in 2026. According to Gospel Hypers, the project features worship, praise, contemporary gospel and Afrogospel styles, including the song Omewoya with Nigerian gospel musician Judikay, focusing on faith, worship and gratitude.

Following her album launch, Yurglee took her ministry overseas through a European tour before traveling to East Africa in August 2026. Gospel Hypers stated that she ministered as a guest artiste at the Daughters of Zion Convention in Nairobi, Kenya, leading attendees in worship.

Lady Bishop Kathy Kiuna hosted the event at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre under the theme Raising the Standard Among Women. Gospel Hypers reported that the gathering brought female believers, church leaders and ministers together for spiritual teaching, fellowship, worship and empowerment.

Yurglee also gained national acclaim at the 2024 Telecel Ghana Music Awards, where her single Hold My Hands earned her the award for Best Female Vocal Performance.

Other Ghanaian gospel performers were also recognized at the 2026 CLIMA Africa event, according to Gospel Hypers. Musician Piesie Esther secured the continental female artiste award and won the best gospel video title for her song Nyame Ye.

Artist Scott Evans received the continental urban gospel award. Gospel Hypers added that several Nigerian gospel performers also featured prominently among the winners, picking up accolades across multiple categories.
