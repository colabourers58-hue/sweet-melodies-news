Swedru All Blacks defeat Asante Kotoko in Premier League

A stoppage-time penalty from Wiafe Darkwah secures a 1-0 victory over Asante Kotoko in week six of the Ghana Premier League.

Swedru All Blacks defeated Asante Kotoko 1-0 in their week six Ghana Premier League match following a dramatic late penalty kick.

Why it matters

The victory earns Swedru All Blacks three vital points while handing Asante Kotoko an early setback in the league. For families, workers and football supporters across Ghana, the dramatic home outcome provides a major talking point in the ongoing season.

The details

The domestic league match remained goalless throughout regular playing time before a late penalty kick in added time ultimately decided the outcome between the two clubs.

The Ghana Football Association was the first outlet to publish the result, reporting that Wiafe Darkwah converted a last-gasp penalty to sink Asante Kotoko and secure a vital win for Swedru All Blacks.

In its coverage of the game, GhanaSoccernet confirmed that the one-nil victory earned the home side all three maximum points in week six of the ongoing 2026/27 top-flight league season.

Ghana Sports Online highlighted in its match report that the late victory allowed Swedru All Blacks to maintain their established dominance over Asante Kotoko in Premier League fixtures.

SportsWorldGhana added that the stoppage-time penalty stunned visiting Asante Kotoko, who were on the brink of securing a draw after keeping their opponents at bay until the dying moments of the match.

For football lovers, traders, students, church members and families following top-flight sports across Ghana and the diaspora, the dramatic finish formed a major talking point from the round of matches.

The defeat delivers an unexpected early setback to Asante Kotoko, while Swedru All Blacks earn a massive boost from defeating one of the country’s most prominent football clubs.

Both teams will now shift their focus to their respective week seven encounters as the 2026/27 Ghana Premier League campaign continues across various venues nationwide.

What’s next

Both clubs will now prepare for their respective week seven fixtures in the ongoing 2026/27 Ghana Premier League season.
