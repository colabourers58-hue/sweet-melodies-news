Assemblies of God loans 10,000-seater dome for Ghana at 70 celebrations

Shipping delays from Dubai prevented the church from using the facility for its ongoing global congress in Accra.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Assemblies of God Ghana will lend its newly acquired ten-thousand seat dome facility to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for state activities celebrating Ghana’s 70th anniversary.

Why it matters

The temporary facility provides additional venue capacity for government functions during the country’s upcoming 70th independence anniversary activities in Accra. It highlights civic cooperation between Ghana’s Christian community and state institutions.

The details

Citi Newsroom first reported the announcement, which the General Superintendent of the church, Rev. Dr Stephen Yenusom Wengnam, disclosed in the Greater Accra Region on Wednesday, 14 October 2026.

According to Adom News, Rev. Dr Wengnam shared the development during the launch of the global denomination’s tenth congress at the Accra International Conference Centre Dome.

The church leader explained that organizers had originally planned to gather at Independence Square. To host delegates comfortably, church leadership ordered the custom 10,000-seater dome from Dubai, but unexpected technical challenges delayed its arrival in the country.

Although the structure has reached the port, Rev. Dr Wengnam stated that technicians require roughly 10 days to erect it. That required setup period made the structure unavailable for the ongoing international conference.

MyJoyOnline reported that the church decided to put the imported facility to another beneficial use. The denomination is relocating the structure to its Assemblies of God Centenary City property before handing it over for state anniversary programs.

In his address, Rev. Dr Wengnam said, “We are moving it to the Assemblies of God Centenary City land, and then we’ll loan it to Foreign Affairs for the Ghana at 70 events.”

Meanwhile, delegates and worshipers are gathering for evening revival rallies at the Accra Sports Stadium. These daily outdoor services open at 5 p.m. and will conclude on Friday, 16 October 2026.

What’s next

The 10th World Assemblies of God Congress continues with nightly revival services at the Accra Sports Stadium through Friday, 16 October 2026.
