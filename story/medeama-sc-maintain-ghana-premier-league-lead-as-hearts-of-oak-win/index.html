Medeama SC maintain Ghana Premier League lead as Hearts of Oak win

Medeama SC opened a three-point lead at the top of the Ghana Premier League table after matchday six victories.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Medeama SC defeated Heart of Lions to stay unbeaten and top the Ghana Premier League after matchday six fixtures.

Why it matters

Medeama SC established a three-point advantage at the top of the Ghana Premier League table after maintaining their unbeaten start. Hearts of Oak’s victory ended a winless streak and moved them into second position.

The details

Reporting on the 2026/27 season matchday six fixtures, 3News stated that Medeama cruised past Heart of Lions to keep hold of the top spot. SportsWorldGhana reported that Medeama’s win over Heart of Lions allowed the club to open a three-point lead at the summit.

The Ghana News Agency noted that Medeama remain unbeaten after six weeks of competition. The agency detailed how Medeama’s steady performance has kept them at the top of the table as the league season gains momentum.

In another matchday six encounter, Accra Hearts of Oak defeated Port City FC 1-0. SportsWorldGhana reported that the single-goal victory enabled Hearts of Oak to put an end to a troubling three-game winless run in the competition.

According to the Ghana News Agency, the victory over Port City FC propelled Hearts of Oak into second place in the league table. Outlets including Ghana Sports Page and Modern Ghana provided live coverage of the match as Hearts of Oak claimed all three points.

Modern Ghana reported in its matchday six wrap-up that Medeama SC’s victory over Heart of Lions and Hearts of Oak’s win against Port City FC were central results in the latest round of Ghana Premier League fixtures.

The latest results mean Medeama SC hold a three-point advantage at the top, while Hearts of Oak sit second. Sports fans across Ghana and the diaspora continue to monitor the progress of the 2026/27 top-flight season.
