Tribe Culture Fest CEO denies World Cup fund allegations by Kofi Adams

The event organiser has challenged the sports minister to substantiate claims or retract comments about missing tournament money.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

The Chief Executive Officer of Tribe Culture Fest has challenged politician Kofi Adams to substantiate or withdraw allegations regarding missing 2026 World Cup funds.

Why it matters

For families and workers across Ghana, this dispute touches on accountability for public funds spent on major international sporting events. It also highlights potential legal action between event organisers and government officials.

The details

According to a news report published by GhanaWeb, Mr Adams asserted that state officials could not trace a featured influencer linked to six hundred and ten thousand dollars allocated for national tournament activities.

Modern Ghana reported that the festival chief executive firmly denied the allegations made by the sports minister, which concerned a sum of six hundred and eleven thousand dollars intended for sports promotion.

GhanaSoccernet and GBC Ghana Online reported that the event executive asked Mr Adams to either produce concrete proof backing the claim or issue an immediate statement of withdrawal to the public.

Asaase Radio reported that the event leader insisted on an explicit retraction and apology, cautioning that legal counsel would be instructed to initiate a lawsuit if the demand is ignored.

Discrepancies emerged across local news outlets regarding the exact figure in question, as GhanaWeb cited six hundred and ten thousand dollars while other outlets published six hundred and eleven thousand dollars.

For families, churchgoers, workers and students across Ghana, the disagreement highlights broad public concern over the transparent management of public funds dedicated to major national sporting campaigns.

No official response or counter-statement from Mr Adams was included in any of the available news reports published by the local media houses.

The next step depends on whether Mr Adams provides evidence to support his claims or responds to the festival chief’s demand for an apology and retraction.

What’s next

The festival chief has demanded an apology and retraction, warning that legal action will follow if no proof is provided.
