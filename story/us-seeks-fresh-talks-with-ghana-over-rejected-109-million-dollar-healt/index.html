US seeks fresh talks with Ghana over rejected 109 million dollar health deal

American officials want to modify controversial clauses that led Cabinet to turn down a five-year healthcare assistance package.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

The United States has requested fresh negotiations with Ghana to review a rejected 109 million dollar health assistance agreement, President John Mahama announced.

Why it matters

Healthcare funding directly affects medical supplies, disease tracking, and safety standards at public hospitals serving Ghanaian families and workers. Cabinet’s decision to renegotiate aims to protect national authority over health data and drug inspections.

The details

Speaking during a public citizens’ engagement held in Goaso, located in the Ahafo Region, President Mahama revealed that American officials asked for talks to reconsider specific clauses that Cabinet had rejected, MyJoyOnline reported.

The proposed five-year arrangement was designed to deliver approximately 109 million dollars in health support to Ghana, taking the place of funding previously obtained through USAID, according to MyJoyOnline.

Ghana turned down the deal following standard official procedures. The Ministry of Health conducted a thorough review of the compact draft and presented an information paper outlining problematic provisions to Cabinet.

Major objections involved national sovereignty, required counterpart funding, and demands for sensitive health data. The agreement required Ghana to provide its pathogen profile mapping local disease-causing organisms and hand over medical records.

Speaking at a New York forum on Friday, 25 September, President Mahama questioned why any foreign country would ask for another nation’s medical records, MyJoyOnline reported.

The President further noted that the draft deal required Ghana to contribute its own funds while restricting the Food and Drugs Authority from inspecting imported drugs and medical supplies, calling the proposed terms humiliating.

Cabinet originally decided not to proceed under those conditions. However, President Mahama confirmed Ghanaian representatives will now sit down with the American team to see if contested clauses can be altered or deleted.

What’s next

Ghanaian officials and American delegates will meet for further discussions to evaluate whether the contested clauses can be modified or removed.
