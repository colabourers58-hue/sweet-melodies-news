Bishop Obinim denies arrest and threat claims as divorce dispute grows

The founder of International God’s Way Church has publicly rejected allegations that he threatened his estranged wife, gospel musician Florence Obinim.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Bishop Daniel Obinim has publicly denied allegations of threatening his estranged wife and addressed reports regarding his arrest following their ongoing divorce proceedings.

Why it matters

The public fallout between two prominent Christian figures in Ghana has sparked widespread debate across congregations and online platforms. It highlights how high-profile marital disputes can affect public perception of religious leadership.

The details

Speaking during a church service broadcast on OB TV from Tema in the Greater Accra Region, the founder of International God’s Way Church rejected claims that he threatened to harm gospel singer Florence Obinim or destroy her vehicle.

Yen.com.gh reported that social media rumors alleged police took the pastor into custody following a confrontation at her residence. His junior pastor, Reverend Kwabena Okyere, denied the arrest report and confirmed the bishop remained at home.

The gospel musician’s brother, Alfred Manu, offered a different account to reporters at the Lakeside Police CID headquarters, according to Yen.com.gh. He alleged that the bishop sent representatives to warn his sister to remove a gifted vehicle or face its destruction.

During the Tema broadcast, Bishop Obinim also addressed the financial and property aspects of their divorce, which follows 24 years of marriage.

News outlets reported conflicting details regarding the monetary demands in the legal documents. Yen.com.gh reported that the filing seeks GH¢20 million in addition to GH¢10,000 monthly for medical care, whereas Adom News stated the request was for GH¢2 million in damages alongside GH¢10,000 weekly for upkeep.

The reported property claims include a residence in Barcelona, a guest house in Accra, a commercial block in Tema, and the church building in Tema, according to Yen.com.gh and Adom News. Bishop Obinim told his congregation that he would contest the claims legally.

What’s next

The divorce proceedings are expected to continue in court as both parties prepare to contest the property and financial claims.
