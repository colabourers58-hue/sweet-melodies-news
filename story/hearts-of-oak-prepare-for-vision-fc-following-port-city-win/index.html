Hearts of Oak Prepare for Vision FC Following Port City Win

Coach Nebojsa Kapor calls on supporters as Vision FC aim to end a five-match winless streak.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Accra Hearts of Oak head coach Nebojsa Kapor has demanded improved preparation from his players ahead of their upcoming Ghana Premier League fixture against Vision FC.

Why it matters

Hearts of Oak are looking to build momentum after ending a three-game winless run with a narrow victory over Port City. Meanwhile, Vision FC face mounting pressure as they enter the fixture on a five-game winless streak.

The details

GhanaSoccernet reported that Coach Kapor credited the squad’s internal mentality for helping the club return to winning ways in their recent domestic outing. SportsWorldGhana reported that this return to victory came through a narrow win against Port City, a match that featured the debut appearance of player Prince Amevor.

Addressing the team’s recent form, Coach Kapor emphasized that a preceding three-game winless run had not weakened the confidence of the squad, according to GhanaSoccernet. Nevertheless, the head coach warned that his technical team and players must prepare differently for their next match following a defeat in Tema.

To build momentum for the upcoming match, Coach Kapor appealed directly to the club’s supporters. GhanaSoccernet reported that the coach urged Hearts of Oak fans to turn out in large numbers at the stadium to support the team against Vision FC.

Vision FC enters the contest under pressure after failing to secure a win in their last five league matches, SportsWorldGhana reported. The upcoming match presents a critical test for the side as they seek to break their winless stretch against Accra Hearts of Oak.

Despite their recent struggles, Wilson Asare Burton insisted that Vision FC must beat Hearts of Oak in what he described as a must-win encounter, according to SportsWorldGhana. Both teams now prepare for the clash with contrasting momentum and urgent points at stake.

What’s next

The two clubs will face each other in their upcoming Ghana Premier League fixture.
