Finance Minister Dr Cassiel Ato Forson leads delegation to IMF meetings in Bangkok

Ghana is seeking foreign investment for jobs and infrastructure after transitioning from a 3 billion dollar IMF bailout to a non-financing policy agreement.

Sweet Melodies News
WhatsAppX Facebook

Finance Minister Dr Cassiel Ato Forson is heading Ghana’s delegation to the IMF and World Bank annual gathering in Bangkok, Thailand, beginning Monday, 12 October.

Why it matters

For Ghanaian families, workers and graduates, the government’s investment drive aims to create local manufacturing jobs and improve infrastructure rather than relying strictly on raw commodity exports. The talks in Bangkok focus on attracting private capital to support domestic trade, energy networks and affordable housing.

The details

According to MyJoyOnline, Bank of Ghana Governor Dr Johnson Asiama and top representatives from the treasury and central bank joined the minister for the weeklong conference.

The nation finished its final review under a 3 billion dollar Extended Credit Facility during July 2026, transitioning into a 36-month Policy Coordination Instrument focused on maintaining fiscal stability without requesting direct loans.

The High Street Journal reported that delegates are presenting investment proposals for electrical grids, transport logistics and industrial hubs under the 24-Hour Economy model. The pitch also covers mixed-finance structures for low-cost housing alongside expanded credit for local manufacturing and processing businesses.

Latest economic data indicates headline inflation was 5.2 per cent for September 2026, while commercial interest benchmarks softened as the Ghana Reference Rate dropped to 10.04 per cent in October, according to The High Street Journal.

3News reported that Dr Forson explained this policy direction during a speech at the University for Development Studies Nyankpala campus in the Northern Region. He noted that earlier periods of economic stability did not yield adequate employment opportunities for educated youth across the country.

Dr Forson explained that the upcoming policy focus aims to build domestic capacity, stating, “It is about building an economy that produces more of what we consume.”

MyJoyOnline reported that Dr Asiama stated Ghana will utilize the summit to refine program benchmarks while collaborating with fellow African nations to advocate for global financial system reforms.

What’s next

Dr Forson is expected to formally present the full New Economy blueprint to Parliament during the 2027 Budget presentation in November.
