Verse of the dayWednesday 14 October 2026

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.”

Proverbs 3:5-6 King James Version

Share on WhatsApp Facebook

Reflection: Trusting God’s Guidance

King Solomon wrote these proverbs to teach young people in Israel how to live wisely under God’s covenant. Human wisdom is limited because we can only see what is directly in front of us.

You may be sitting in a trotro near the Kwame Nkrumah Interchange, worrying about money or family troubles. Perhaps you are a trader at Kejetia market, wondering how to pay your supplier this week. When you “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart”, you hand over those worries to God. You can look at Psalm 37:5 to see how God guides those who rely on Him.

Take time today to speak to God about your daily tasks. Seek His guidance in your work and family decisions before taking your next step.

Pray

Heavenly Father, help us to rely completely on Your wisdom today and guide every step we take in Jesus’ name. Amen.
