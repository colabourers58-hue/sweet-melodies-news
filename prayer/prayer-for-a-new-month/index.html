Prayer for a new month

Pray this prayer on the first morning of the month to give thanks for past blessings and dedicate your upcoming plans to God.

Father, I thank you for bringing me safely into this new month. Thank you for keeping my family through the weeks that have just ended. Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness. This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

I bring my daily work and my home before you. Protect my family when we board the trotro or sit at our work desks. Lord, I hand over my specific worry to you today, especially [name your personal concern here]. Grant me wisdom to make honest decisions and patience to love those around me.

Help me to grow in faith each day and walk closely with you. Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert. Keep my spirit quiet when difficult times come. I place my future in your hands.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
