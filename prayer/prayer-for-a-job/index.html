Prayer for a job

Pray this prayer in the morning before you go out to search for work or attend an interview.

Heavenly Father, I come to You today seeking employment. As I wake up each morning to look for work, my heart carries worries about rent and daily expenses. You see every application I submit and every trotro ride I take to check on openings. I commit my works unto the LORD, and my thoughts shall be established.

Specifically, I am asking You today for [mention the exact job or interview you are preparing for]. Open right doors that fit the skills You gave me. Grant me clear speech and calm confidence when I sit before interviewers. Let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us, and establish the work of our hands.

Give me patience while I wait. Help me to use my talents well every day, serving others at home or in my neighborhood. I rest in Your promise that my God shall supply all my need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.

I place my future into Your loving hands. Fill me with peace and steady my heart. Establish thou the work of our hands upon us. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
