Prayer for comfort in grief

Pray this prayer when you are grieving the loss of a loved one and need God’s comfort.

Father, I come to You with a heavy heart because death has taken someone I love dearly. Today I bring before You my sorrow over [name the person you are grieving]. Lord, Your word says that the LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

Please comfort my family as we sit together at home. Give us strength for the funeral service and for the hard weeks after the burial, when the quietness returns. Stay close to us at work, in the market, on the trotro, and inside our quiet rooms. Hold us up when sadness feels too heavy to carry.

I hold fast to the hope of the resurrection through Jesus Christ. I thank You because blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. I look forward to the promise that God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain. Keep my family safe in Your peace. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
